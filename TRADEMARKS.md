This policy is intended to let the community accurately discuss and extend ShotForward while preventing confusion about which products and services are official.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may:

- truthfully state that software, a plugin, a template, a tutorial, or a service is designed for, compatible with, or based on ShotForward;
- link to the official ShotForward repository or website;
- use the ShotForward name in ordinary commentary, reviews, news, academic work, and community discussion;
- retain copyright, license, and attribution notices required by the applicable software license; and
- describe an unmodified official release as ShotForward when you make clear where it came from and do not imply that you are the official distributor or service operator.

Where practical, use wording such as "for ShotForward", "compatible with ShotForward", or "based on ShotForward" rather than using ShotForward as the name of your product or service.

You must obtain written permission before you:

- use ShotForward or a confusingly similar term as the primary name of a company, application, hosted service, package, marketplace, event, or domain;
- use the official logo, application icon, or visual identity as your own product branding;
- describe a modified build as an official ShotForward release;
- imply sponsorship, certification, partnership, or endorsement by ShotForward;
- register a company name, domain, social account, application listing, or trademark containing a ShotForward Mark; or
- use a ShotForward Mark in advertising in a way that may confuse users about the source of a product or service.

## Modified builds and forks

A publicly distributed modified build or fork must be clearly identified as unofficial and must use a distinct product name and visual identity unless ShotForward has given written permission. Required copyright and origin notices must remain intact. Factual text such as "Based on ShotForward" may be used in accordance with this policy.

The right to use software under Apache-2.0 does not include the right to present a fork, hosted service, or derivative product as ShotForward.

## Plugins and integrations

Community plugins and integrations may use a phrase such as "Compatible with ShotForward". They may not call themselves "official", use the official logo as their primary icon, or imply review or certification unless ShotForward has provided written authorization.

This policy does not limit rights that applicable trademark law permits without authorization. To request permission or report confusing use, contact **hi@shotforward.com** with the proposed name, artwork, context, territory, and duration of use.
